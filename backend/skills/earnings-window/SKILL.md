# Earnings window

The next earnings report falls inside the recommendation's horizon. Check that the call is ready for it.

## Steps

1. **Is the report acknowledged?** If it is within 30 days and the recommendation never mentions earnings, that is a major challenge: a single report can move the price more than the whole expected return. Further out, a minor one.
2. **Recent surprises.** The fact sheet gives the beat rate and the latest surprise. A consistent beat record supports a BUY; recent misses challenge it.
3. **Estimate revisions.** Rising current-year estimates (90-day change above zero, more upward than downward revisions in 30 days) support a BUY and challenge a SELL; falling estimates do the opposite. Say which and by how much.
4. **Entry timing.** For a BUY or advice to add, note whether buying before the report adds risk the recommendation hasn't priced in. Don't predict the result of the report.

Large, heavily followed companies show weaker drift; don't overstate the effect for them.
