# Position review

The user holds this stock. Check the position advice.

## Steps

1. **Anchoring.** Advice should rest on the stock's prospects from today's price, not on the purchase price. Flag advice that reasons from the cost basis, such as "hold until it gets back to your average cost" or "take profits because you're up 40%" with no other reason: minor, or major if it's the main argument. Taxes are a legitimate reason to consider cost, but the data doesn't include them.
2. **Concentration.** The fact sheet gives the position's share of the portfolio. Above 20% (a common rule of thumb), advice to add should acknowledge the concentration; if it doesn't, that's a major challenge. Above 10%, mention it.
3. **Consistency.** The position advice should match the recommendation: "add" with HOLD or SELL, or "sell" with BUY, needs an explanation.

If the portfolio share isn't available (prices missing), say so and skip step 2.
